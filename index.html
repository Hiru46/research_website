SLIIT research project / R26-SE-022

An intelligent study assistant for the whole learning journey.

AuraLearn is an AI-enabled intelligent study assistant that turns lecture materials into useful study resources, supports grounded questions, observes learning readiness, and adapts revision reminders to the learner.

01 / Domain

One platform for preparing, understanding, and remembering.

AuraLearn addresses the fragmented study workflow by connecting content processing, grounded assistance, emotion and attention signals, and adaptive revision support in one research prototype.

Literature survey

Intelligent learning support

The project brings together educational summarisation, retrieval-augmented generation, affective computing, and spaced repetition to support the learner across a study cycle.

Research gap

Tools are often disconnected

Summaries, tutors, emotion-aware interfaces, and revision reminders are commonly treated as separate tools. AuraLearn investigates how their outputs can inform one another.

Research problem

How can study support adapt?

The project investigates how learning content, questions, learner state, and review history can be combined to deliver more relevant and timely academic assistance.

Objectives

Support the complete study loop

  • Convert lecture materials into structured study resources.
  • Answer questions using retrieved source knowledge.
  • Recognise emotion and attention-related signals.
  • Personalise reminders and revision activities.
Technologies used

Full-stack research prototype

Next.js and HTML/CSS provide the interfaces; FastAPI and Python services provide APIs; MongoDB stores application state; transformer models, Sentence-BERT, FAISS, MediaPipe, Random Forest, and SM-2 support the research modules.

02 / Research components

Four components, one study experience.

Each component has a distinct research responsibility, while the integrated platform connects their outputs into a learner workflow.

Module 1 / IT22187178

Lecture summarisation

Processes lecture materials and produces concise, high-yield study content.

  • BART/T5 transformer approach
  • Importance ranking
  • KEY, MEDIUM, and LOW information tags
  • Flashcard generation
Module 2 / IT22120120

Hallucination-safe tutor

Uses retrieval before generation so answers remain grounded in the student's uploaded learning materials.

  • Sentence-BERT encoding
  • FAISS vector search
  • Top-k context retrieval
  • Uncertainty scoring
Module 3 / IT22224552

Emotion detection

Analyses facial and eye-behaviour signals during study sessions to estimate learning-relevant affect and attention.

  • MediaPipe Face Mesh
  • 468 landmark points
  • Eye Aspect Ratio and blink analysis
  • Five-class emotion output
Module 4 / IT22371690

Emotion and progress-aware reminders

Combines readiness, memory retention, review urgency, and topic context to decide when and how to prompt revision.

  • Readiness model and safety rules
  • SM-2 spaced repetition engine
  • Adaptive send, delay, and skip decisions
  • Personalised activity recommendations
03 / Architecture and methodology

From uploaded material to adaptive study action.

The platform is organised as a sequence of services. Content becomes searchable study knowledge, learner state becomes an interpretable signal, and the reminder component closes the loop through feedback.

01

Upload material

Lecture PDFs and PPTX files enter the learning workspace.

02

Summarise

Important content becomes summaries, tags, and flashcards.

03

Ask grounded questions

Retrieval brings relevant source chunks to the AI tutor.

04

Monitor study state

Emotion and attention cues inform readiness and intervention.

05

Revise adaptively

Retention and feedback guide the next reminder and activity.

Frontend

Next.js experience

Dashboard, study workspace, authentication, focus views, reminder queue, and project demonstrations.

Backend services

Python APIs

FastAPI services coordinate authentication, content processing, tutoring, emotion inputs, reminders, and evaluation routes.

Data and models

Research infrastructure

MongoDB stores learner and reminder state; vector search, ML models, SM-2, and evaluation scripts support the research modules.

Reminder module spotlight

The reminder component is one part of the integrated system. It receives learner-state and content context, then returns an explainable revision decision and activity recommendation.
